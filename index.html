A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Good to know

Where can I find the daily aarti timings?

Morning and evening timings are shown at the top of this page. The programme section lists special events. Timings in this preview are samples until confirmed by the mandal.

How can I volunteer or become a sponsor?

Use the contact form and select “Join as a volunteer” or “Advertise or sponsor”. You can review and share your message with the mandal once the official contact is added.

Can I share photographs from previous years?

Yes. Select “Share a memory or guest visit” in the contact form and describe the year and occasion. Share photographs you own or have permission to publish with the mandal team.
